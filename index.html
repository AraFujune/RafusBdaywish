My Beautiful Wife

I don’t think I realized when I first met you just how special you would become to me. But slowly, talking to you, hearing your voice, seeing your smile, and learning all your little habits became such an important part of my days. At some point, I realized that my days felt incomplete when I didn’t talk to you, and somehow, even your smallest smile could make my entire day better. That’s when I think I truly understood that you weren’t just someone I loved—you had become the one I wanted beside me.

Today, what I love most about you isn’t just how beautiful you are, but the person you are. I love the way you care jaan, the little things you remember, your smile, your anger, your little moments of being upset, and even all the silly things you do. You probably don’t realize how much your presence has changed my life. With you, I can be completely myself. Even a simple “what are you doing?” from you can put a smile on my face. And honestly, I don’t need a specific reason to love you. I love you because you are who you are, and that has always been enough for me.

On your birthday, my biggest wish is that you always stay happy, that every dream you have slowly becomes a reality, and that no matter how difficult life gets, you never lose the beautiful light inside you. And if life gives me the chance, I want to always be one of the reasons behind your smile. A new year of your life is beginning, and I hope I get to be beside you through all the beautiful moments, the difficult ones, and everything in between. Happy Birthday, my love, my jaan, my pakhi, my Rafuu, my shonaaa. I want to love you today, tomorrow, and through every tomorrow that comes after. ❤️

Yours One and Only Husband
